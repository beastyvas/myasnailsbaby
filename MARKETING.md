# Growth Playbook — Mya's Nails Baby

What the site does on its own, and what only you can do. The first section is
the highest-value hour you will ever spend on this business.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile — do this first

For a nail studio, the Business Profile outranks the website itself. When
someone in Vegas searches "nails near me", Google shows a map with three
listings above every website result. Getting into that box is worth more than
any amount of SEO on the site.

**Set it up:** [google.com/business](https://google.com/business), signed in
with the Google account that should own it — use one you'll keep forever, not
a personal throwaway.

Copy these in exactly. They must match the website **character for character**
— Google cross-checks the name, address and phone (your "NAP") across the web,
and mismatches quietly cost you ranking.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | `Mya's Nails Baby` |
| Category (primary) | **Nail salon** |
| Categories (secondary) | Nail technician, Beauty salon |
| Address | `2080 E. Flamingo Rd. Suite #106, Room 4, Las Vegas, NV 89119` |
| Phone | `(702) 981-8428` |
| Website | `https://www.myasnailsbaby.com` |
| Booking link | `https://www.myasnailsbaby.com/#booking` |

**Hours** — match the site exactly:

| Day | |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Tuesday | 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Wednesday | Closed |
| Thursday | Closed |
| Friday | 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM |
| Saturday | 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM |
| Sunday | Closed |

**Services** — add each one so they show as their own line on the listing:
Gel-X Extensions, Acrylic Nails, Hard Gel, Builder Gel Manicure, Gel Manicure,
Custom Nail Art, Gel Pedicure.

**Description** — paste this. It uses the words people actually search:

> Custom nail sets by Mya in Las Vegas. Gel-X, acrylic, hard gel, builder gel
> and hand-painted nail art, plus gel pedicures. By appointment only, in a
> private suite on E. Flamingo Rd. Book online with a $20 deposit that goes
> toward your set.

**Then:**

1. Verify (usually a video walkthrough of the suite — have the door number
   and your tools visible).
2. Upload photos. **This is the part people skip and it matters enormously.**
   Ten or more of your best sets, plus the suite itself. Add a few every week.
   Listings with fresh photos get meaningfully more clicks than dormant ones.
3. Turn on messaging so people can text you from the listing.
4. Post an "Update" weekly — a new set, an opening this week, a promo. Active
   profiles rank above dormant ones.

## 2. Reviews — already automatic

Reviews are the number one thing deciding where you land in that map box, and
the number one thing deciding whether someone picks you once they see it.

**The site already handles the asking.** Two hours after every appointment
ends, the client gets a thank-you text that asks for a Google review and tells
them exactly how to find you. Two hours is deliberate — it's while they're
still looking at their hands and loving them.

You don't need to do anything for this to work. Switch it off any time under
Settings → Automatic Texts.

There's no link in it on purpose: link-free texts don't get filtered by
carriers, and it works before your profile even has a short link.

**What you can add on top:** once the profile is live, Business Profile has an
"Ask for reviews" button that gives you a short link. Send that personally to
your ten favourite clients. A personal ask converts better than any automation.

**Target: 20 reviews.** That's roughly the point where a local listing starts
holding its own in a competitive city.

## 3. Rebooking — already automatic

The single biggest lever on income for a nail tech isn't new clients, it's the
same clients coming back on schedule.

Three weeks after someone's last set, if they've got nothing booked, the site
texts them that they're about due and offers to save them a spot. Three weeks
is when sets genuinely start growing out, so it lands as helpful rather than
pushy — and it catches them **at full price**, weeks before they'd drift far
enough to need a discount to come back.

Change the timing or switch it off under Settings → Automatic Texts.

---

## 4. Reactivation — one tap

For the ones who slipped through anyway: Dashboard → **Reactivate**. Anyone
45 days quiet with nothing booked shows up there. Tick who you want, send, and
each gets their own percent-off code good for 30 days.

If they book from that number inside the window it's credited automatically —
you get a text so you know to honour it at the chair.

Run it when you have a quiet week ahead. Don't run it every week; an offer
that always exists stops being an offer.

## 5. Instagram → bookings

Your IG is the top of the funnel and the site is the bottom. The handoff is
the bio link.

- **Bio link:** `https://www.myasnailsbaby.com` — straight to booking. Don't
  send people to a Linktree; every extra tap loses people.
- **Bio formula:** `Nail Artist | Las Vegas 💅 Gel-X · Acrylic · Custom Art |
  Book below ⬇️`
- **What converts:** before/afters, close-ups in natural light, and
  hands-moving video. Static flat-lays get likes; video gets bookings.
- **Every caption ends with a CTA.** "Booking link in bio" — every time, even
  when it feels repetitive. It doesn't to them; they see one post, not all of
  them.
- **Tag the location** on every post. Geotagged posts feed local discovery.

## 6. Abandoned checkouts — already automatic

Someone picks a time, gets to the card form, and stops. It happens constantly
on phones, and they'd already decided — they just got interrupted.

Half an hour later they get one text saying the slot is still open. One only,
never a second, and never if they've already booked something else.

## 7. What's already handled in code

You don't need to think about any of this — noted so you know it exists.

- Appointment reminders 24 hours ahead and again on the day.
- New-booking alerts texted to you the moment someone pays, plus a text when
  someone cancels or reschedules so you can fill the gap.
- If two people somehow pay for the same slot, the second is refunded
  automatically *and told* — so nobody turns up to an appointment that was
  never made.
- `NailSalon` structured data, so Google can read your hours, address,
  services and price range directly off the site.
- FAQ structured data — the deposit, location, services, late and cancellation
  policy questions can appear straight in search results.
- Sitemap and robots at `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt`.
- Link previews (`/api/og`) so the site looks right when shared in a DM.

**One thing to do after deploying:** submit
`https://www.myasnailsbaby.com/sitemap.xml` at
[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console). It's free
and it's how Google learns the site exists rather than waiting to stumble on
it.
